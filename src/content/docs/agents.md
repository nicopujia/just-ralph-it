# AI Agents

Just Ralph It runs on two AI agents that have different jobs. Think of them as a conversation and a construction crew.

Ralphy asks the questions. Ralph does the building.

Neither agent can do the other's job well, and both are necessary. You cannot build well from vague instructions, and clarifying an idea does not help if nothing gets built afterward.

## Ralphy - The Interviewer

Ralphy is your conversation partner at the start of every project. Its job is to understand what you actually want, not just what you said.

Most people describe their ideas in broad strokes. Ralphy digs deeper. It asks follow-up questions about things you did not think to mention. It clarifies ambiguous requirements. It surfaces edge cases and asks how they should be handled. It identifies gaps between what you said and what you implied.

By the end of this conversation, Ralphy has turned a rough idea into a clear, detailed plan of action. This plan is what the build phase works from.

Ralphy is the reason Just Ralph It does not waste your time building the wrong thing. It is not a fast process and it is not meant to be. Rushing through clarification is how software gets built that does not match what the customer wanted.

## Ralph - The Builder

Ralph takes the clarified plan from Ralphy and turns it into working software.

Ralph works through tasks one at a time. It does not try to do everything at once. Each task is a specific piece of functionality: a feature, a bug fix, a UI component, a configuration. Ralph implements each task, tests it, and moves to the next.

As Ralph works, you can see what it is doing in real time. You are never left wondering whether the build is progressing. When a task is complete, you can review it, ask for changes, or move on.

When Ralph finishes all the tasks in the plan, the project is complete. Depending on your settings, it may be deployed automatically, or held for your review.

## How they work together

Ralphy and Ralph operate in sequence, not in parallel. Ralphy finishes the interview, produces the task plan, and then Ralph starts building. If you request changes after reviewing what was built, Ralphy can step in again to clarify the revision, and Ralph picks up the new tasks.

This two-step process is the core of what makes Just Ralph It work. Ralphy makes sure the destination is correct. Ralph navigates the route. Skipping either step produces worse results.

## Working with tasks

As Ralphy clarifies your project, it breaks your idea down into a list of concrete tasks. Each task represents a unit of work that can be built and verified independently. You can review this task list, add details, or suggest changes before Ralph begins.

During the build, tasks are marked as pending, in progress, or complete. You always know exactly where the project stands. If something needs adjusting, you can add new tasks or refine existing ones.

This task-based structure means nothing gets lost. Every requirement, no matter how small, lives as a task that either gets done or gets explicitly skipped. There is no ambiguity about whether something was forgotten or simply not mentioned.
